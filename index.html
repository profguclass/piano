Lessons

The levels and their parts (technique, pieces, ear tests and sight reading) follow the RCM Piano Syllabus (2022 edition), from Preparatory A to Level 6. Technique and pieces have three steps: listen, Wait for me, then Play along at the target tempo. Finger numbers: 1 = thumb, 2 = index, 3 = middle, 4 = ring, 5 = little finger.

Library

Practise a piece as a whole, from the first bar to the last, without the lesson steps. Choose Listen, Play along or Wait for me, the hands and the tempo as usual; your progress is saved for each piece. Add your own MusicXML scores (.musicxml, .xml, .mxl) and they stay in My scores on this device.
